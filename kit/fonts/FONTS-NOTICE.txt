  (https://github.com/googlefonts/AntonFont)

Montserrat-Heavy.woff (used as "Hook Montserrat")
  Montserrat, Copyright 2011 The Montserrat Project Authors
  (https://github.com/JulietaUla/Montserrat)

Inter-Heavy.woff (used as "Hook Inter")
  Inter, Copyright 2016 The Inter Project Authors
  (https://github.com/rsms/inter)

These three are used by the Hook covers templates and are also licensed under the SIL Open Font License,
Version 1.1, with the full text beside this file: OFL-Anton.txt, OFL-Montserrat.txt and OFL-Inter.txt.
They are the Google Fonts builds, subset to latin and saved as WOFF; Montserrat is limited to weights 700 to 900
and Inter to weights 700 to 900 at its display optical size. Anton's line metrics are tightened
to an ascent of 1900 and a descent of 330 units, so stacked condensed lines measure the way they look.
Nothing else was changed.
